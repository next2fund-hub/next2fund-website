FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about working with Next 2 Fund.

Do I need perfect credit to apply? ▾

No. We work with business owners across the full credit spectrum. We look at the complete picture of your business — revenue, time in business, cash flow, and more — not just your credit score.

How long does the application take? ▾

Our initial application takes less than 5 minutes to complete online. A funding specialist will follow up within 24 business hours to discuss your options and next steps.

Does applying affect my credit score? ▾

The initial application only requires a soft credit pull, which does not impact your credit score. A hard inquiry only occurs when you formally apply with a specific lender, which we'll always notify you about first.

What types of businesses do you work with? ▾

We work with businesses across virtually every industry — from restaurants and retail to construction, healthcare, and tech. We require a minimum of 6 months in business and $5,000/month in revenue to qualify for most products.

What are your fees? ▾

Our consultation and matching services are completely free to you. We're compensated by our lending partners, which means there's no cost for you to use our platform — and no hidden fees.
